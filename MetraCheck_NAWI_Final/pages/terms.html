DEMONSTRATION LEGAL TEMPLATE

Terms & Conditions

These sample terms describe intended use of the MetraCheck prototype. They are not issued by a government authority, are not legal advice, and must be reviewed and replaced by the responsible organization before deployment.

Template date: 26 September 2026

1. Prototype scope

MetraCheck is a front-end demonstration of workflows for Non-Automatic Weighing Instrument (NAWI) product records, laboratory testing, review, and report lookup. It is not a production service and does not itself approve products, certify instruments, or determine legal conformity.

2. Account responsibility and authorized access

In a production deployment, users would be responsible for protecting their credentials, using accounts only for their assigned duties, and promptly reporting suspected unauthorized access. This prototype has no real authentication, role enforcement, or identity verification. Do not enter real credentials or confidential information.

3. Data accuracy and assessment decisions

Users of a future production system would be responsible for the accuracy and completeness of records they submit, subject to applicable organizational procedures. Demo readings, tolerances, pass/fail outcomes, and sample manufacturer information are illustrative only. They have not been validated against a specific instrument, approval, jurisdiction, edition of OIML R-76, or applicable national requirements.

4. Digital records, reports, and verification

Reports and verification identifiers generated by this prototype are local demonstration records. A matching report identifier is not proof of authenticity, legal validity, approval, or current status. Production records would require an authority-controlled source of truth, integrity safeguards, retention rules, access controls, and a defined process for corrections and revocation.

5. Audit trails

The prototype displays sample audit events and stores some interactions in browser local storage. These records can be edited or deleted by the device user and are not tamper-evident. They must not be treated as official evidence or a complete audit trail.

6. Availability and support

No uptime, data persistence, backup, support, or service continuity is guaranteed. Local browser data may be unavailable or lost. Offline sync controls are illustrative and do not upload records to a server.

7. Misuse and changes

Users must not represent demo reports or calculations as official decisions, bypass applicable procedures, or use the prototype to mislead others. A production service would define additional acceptable-use rules. These template terms may be revised before any production deployment; material changes would need to be communicated by the responsible operator.
