Privacy Policy

This sample privacy notice describes the prototype's intended data-handling concepts. It is not an actual government privacy policy or a statement of compliance with any particular privacy law.

Template date: 26 September 2026

1. About this prototype

MetraCheck is a browser-based demonstration. It does not connect to a production identity service, government registry, laboratory information system, or central report server.

2. Information entered

Sample product, test, user, chat, and notification records are supplied to demonstrate workflows. Information you add, including profile details, product data, and test readings, is stored in this browser's local storage. Image previews may be held in browser memory or stored locally as part of the prototype record. Avoid entering personal, confidential, regulated, or operational information.

3. Use and access

A production system would need to define its data controller, lawful purposes, authorized recipients, retention periods, hosting locations, security controls, and procedures for access or correction requests. This prototype implements none of those organizational or legal arrangements.

4. Local storage and deletion

Browser storage is limited to the device and browser profile in use, is not a secure backup, and can be inspected or cleared by the device user. Signing out does not delete prototype records or consent state. Clear this site's browser storage to remove locally saved demo data.

5. Sharing and disclosure

The prototype does not intentionally transmit form data to a MetraCheck server. External font files may be requested from Google Fonts when network access is available. Production deployment would require an approved hosting and third-party services review.

6. Security and retention

Local browser storage is not encrypted by this application and is not protected by server access controls. Demo role selection is not authorization. Do not use the application for real records. No retention period or deletion guarantee is provided.

7. Changes and contact

This sample text must be replaced with a notice approved by the organization responsible for a production system. Privacy questions for a future deployment should be directed to that organization's designated privacy contact.
